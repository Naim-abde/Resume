Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Resume Forge really free?

Yes. Resume Forge is 100% free. Create and download as many resumes as you want as A4 PDFs, with no account and no payment required.

Can I add more than one job or education entry?

Yes. Add and remove as many work experience and education entries as you need. Every change updates the live preview and the PDF instantly.

Can I build my resume in French?

Yes. Resume Forge supports both English and French. Use the language switcher to change the resume section titles to French — for example Expérience professionnelle and Formation.

Can I add a photo to my resume?

Yes. Upload a photo and it appears as a clean, round image in both the live preview and the downloadable PDF.

What format does the resume download in?

Your resume downloads as a clean, printable A4 PDF, ready to share, email or print.
